# 👑 Princess Tanya's Royal Pink & Hogwarts Birthday Celebration ✨

The complete, interactive 3D birthday celebration website created from Stitch.

---

## 🌟 Interactive Features

1. **🎂 3D Three.js Royal Tiara Cake & Golden Snitch:**
   - Real-time 3D rendered multi-tier birthday cake with delicate frosting, gold filigree, pearls, and ruby gemstones.
   - Royal sparkling tiara crown on top.
   - **Golden Snitch** orbiting the cake with flapping wings (Harry Potter tribute).
   - 7 rose-gold flickering candles.
   - **"Blow Out 3D Candles 🕯️"** button: Extinguishes the 3D candle flames in real-time, fires celebratory fanfare chimes and confetti, and displays the celebratory wish banner.
   - **Relight ✨** button lets Tanya make unlimited wishes.
   - Interactive 3D mouse tracking & spin burst on click.

2. **🎶 Synthesized Web Audio Birthday Melody Player:**
   - Plays the full chime melody of *"Happy Birthday dear Tanya"* synthesized directly through browser Web Audio API (zero external MP3 dependencies).
   - Ambient soundboard with cheer, harp arpeggios, spell sounds, and applause.

3. **⏳ Real-Time Countdown to September 29th:**
   - Live Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds countdown ticker with automatic yearly rollover.

4. **📸 Our Magical Memories Polaroid Gallery:**
   - 8 high-resolution curated romantic photographs stored locally in `assets/images/` with online fallback.
   - Clickable polaroid cards opening an interactive keepsake modal with note submission.

5. **💖 Reasons Why I Love You Endless Amounts:**
   - Enchanted parchment cards celebrating Tanya's radiance, Gryffindor bravery, and kindness.

6. **🎈 Princess Tanya's Balloon Pop Party:**
   - 6 floating magical balloons. Pop them to hear realistic sound effects, trigger sparkle bursts, and uncover secret love tokens.

7. **🌟 Starlight Wish Jar:**
   - Cast a birthday wish to add it permanently into Princess Tanya's cosmic jar with star drop animations.

8. **💌 Secret Love Letter Modal:**
   - Royal keepsake parchment modal with wax seal and heartfelt birthday letter.

9. **🎉 Full-Screen Confetti Cannon System:**
   - 2D canvas physics particle confetti engine triggered by header buttons, candle blowouts, and balloon pops.
